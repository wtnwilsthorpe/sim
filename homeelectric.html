📋 GCSE Mark Scheme Summary

1. UK 3-Pin Plug Wiring & Colours

  • Live (Brown): Carries 230 V alternating P.d. Connected to the fuse.
  • Neutral (Blue): Completes the circuit at ~0 V.
  • Earth (Green & Yellow): Safety wire at 0 V connected to metal casing.
  • Brass pins: Hard, corrosion-resistant conductor. Plastic case: Insulator.

2. Fuse vs Circuit Breaker (MCB)

  • Fuse: Thin wire heats up and melts if current exceeds rating (3A, 5A, 13A), breaking the circuit. Must be replaced.
  • Circuit Breaker (MCB): Electromagnet pulls a switch open on surge. Trips faster and can be reset.

3. Earth Wire & Double Insulation

  • Earth Wire: Provides a low-resistance path to ground if Live touches metal casing → causes a large current surge → blows fuse / trips MCB.
  • Double Insulation (Class II ⏼): Plastic outer casing + insulated internal wires. Cannot conduct electricity, so no Earth wire is needed.

4. AC vs DC & Key Equations

UK Mains: 230 V (RMS), 50 Hz AC ($T = 1/f = 0.02\text{ s}$). AC charges oscillate back & forth; DC charges flow steadily in one direction.

P = I × V  |  Q = I × t  |  E = P × t  |  T = 1 / f
