Year 7 Science: Kinetic Energy & Elasticity

All gas particles at the same temperature possess the same average kinetic energy. When particles collide, energy is 100% conserved (elastic collisions) with no speed lost!

💡

Why is the path zig-zag?

Perfume gas molecules don't travel straight across the room. They experience countless random collisions with background air molecules, creating a zigzag path known as Brownian motion.
