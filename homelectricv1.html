Live Wire (Brown) — 230 V

Bottom Right Pin

Appliance Casing & Insulation

Class I vs Class II

Toggle between appliance types to see why some devices do not need an Earth wire:

Double-Insulated Appliance (e.g. Hairdryer, Drill)

2-Core Cable (Live & Neutral ONLY) • Plastic Earth Pin on Plug

  • Layer 1: Internal wires are wrapped in insulating PVC plastic.
  • Layer 2: The entire outer casing is made of non-conducting plastic.
  • Why no Earth wire? Even if a live wire comes loose inside, the plastic outer case cannot conduct electricity, so a user cannot get an electric shock.

AC

Alternating Current: P.d. constantly changes direction (positive to negative 50 times/sec). Used for mains electricity.

DC

Direct Current: P.d. stays in one direction; electrons flow steadily from negative to positive (cells & batteries).

Cheap & simple, but single-use — once the wire melts, it must be manually replaced.

Circuit Breaker / MCB (Electromagnetic):

Trips open much faster using an electromagnet and can be reset with a switch.
