KS3 Forces & Motion Rules

Core Newton's Laws Summary for Lessons

1. Balanced Forces (Net Force = 0 N)

If forces are equal and opposite, the resultant force is 0 N.

  • If the object is stationary, it remains stationary.
  • If the object is moving, it continues moving at a CONSTANT SPEED in a straight line.

2. Unbalanced Forces (Net Force ≠ 0 N)

If forces do not cancel out, a resultant force exists. This causes the object to change speed (accelerate or decelerate).

Acceleration (a) = Net Force (F) ÷ Mass (m)

Common KS3 Misconception!

"Balanced forces make moving objects stop." — FALSE! Friction or drag stops moving objects. Without friction, a moving object with balanced forces keeps moving forever at the exact same speed!
